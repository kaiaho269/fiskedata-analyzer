# Sprint 12 — FA strategy

## Goal
Define what FA is becoming without locking the project to a particular technology or premature commercial scope.

## Work completed
- Added `strategy.md`.
- Defined the mission: personal utility first, generality second, commercialization last.
- Established questions-before-dashboards and analysis-before-presentation as core principles.
- Defined evidence over assumptions and measure-before-optimizing rules.
- Documented the long-term FAQuery concept: natural-language interaction over the analytical engine.
- Defined AI as a language/reasoning layer, not the numerical source of truth.

## What we learned
The durable asset is not Python, Streamlit or DuckDB. It is the analytical model, domain knowledge, reproducibility and trustworthy answers.

## Direction
The immediate priority is a trustworthy analytical foundation. FAQuery and broader commercialization remain later possibilities.

## Result
FA gained a strategic north star without requiring a technology rewrite.
